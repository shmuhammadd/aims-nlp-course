# SemEval task specification

[Course home](../README.md) · [Capstone brief](PROJECT.md) · [Paper guide](SEMEVAL_PAPER.md)

**Selection status:** the instructor has confirmed SemEval as the capstone. The year, task, and required subtasks/language tracks have not yet been specified. This document records that selection; it does not assign a task by default. The task mentioned in the archived course is not automatically the selection for this edition.

Complete the class-wide fields before students begin task-specific development. Then each team adds its identifier and planned system. The [SemEval hub](https://semeval.github.io/) links to edition-specific information; the selected task's organiser page and the selected edition's paper call govern participation.

## Team addendum

Record members and responsibilities; one focused research question; the baseline; the proposed change; two planned ablations; compute limits; the data-access method; and the commands that will train, predict, score, and export. Record official submission receipts only after an actual submission.

The task may be text-only, multilingual, or multimodal. Apply the relevant course methods without adding modalities or external resources that conflict with the shared-task rules.
